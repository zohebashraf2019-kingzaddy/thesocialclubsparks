    Good to Know

    FAQ

    Is The Social Club 21+?

    Yes — The Social Club is 21 and over at all times. Every guest needs a valid, government-issued photo ID at the door.

    RNB Reno is sold out — can I still get in?

    Online tickets are sold out, but a limited number of tickets are released at the door. They're first come, first served — arrive early.

    How do I reserve bottle service?

    Send us a DM on Instagram or submit the inquiry form above with your date and party size. We'll confirm your section and bottle options.

    Can I rent the whole venue?

    Yes. We host private buyouts, especially on Monday and Tuesday when we're closed to the public. Send an inquiry with your guest count and date.

    I'm a promoter or DJ — how do I pitch a night?

    Choose "Promoter / DJ Night" in the form and tell us your concept, audience and past events. Include your Instagram so we can check out your work.

    What are your hours?

    Sunday, Wednesday and Thursday 4 PM – 12 AM. Friday and Saturday 4 PM – 3 AM. Closed Monday and Tuesday (available for private events).
